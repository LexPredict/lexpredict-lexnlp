About LexNLP
============

Purpose
-------

LexNLP provides legal-aware text segmentation, information extraction, and
machine-learning utilities for real contracts, plans, policies, procedures,
and other unstructured legal text.

Its maintained functionality includes:

* sentence, paragraph, page, section, and title segmentation;
* extraction of amounts, money, percentages, ratios, dates, durations,
  conditions, constraints, courts, regulations, citations, and other facts;
* English, German, and Spanish locale components;
* pre-trained segmentation and classification models; and
* utilities for building legal-text clustering and classification workflows.
